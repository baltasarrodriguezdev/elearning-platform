## Prompt de selva

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE reusable game terrain tile, landscape 4:3, rich polished 16-bit pixel art, top-down with slightly oblique scenery. Use supplied image as STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. It must repeat seamlessly vertically: top/bottom edges consistent terrain color and density. No horizon or sky. No roads, paths, text, UI, markers, numbers, characters, start, finish or final building. This is ground for a separately coded wide winding game route: keep the center 70% largely clear with subtle texture, confine taller detailed scenery to the left and right 15% margins, sparse small decorations elsewhere. No obstacles or water dividing the central walkable ground. Theme: lush tropical jungle. Warm mossy green forest floor with ochre soil highlights, palms, giant ferns, vines, small weathered wooden platforms, broken stone totems, tiny blue ponds and waterfalls confined to outermost edges. Evoke a classic barrel-and-banana platform adventure, with rich emerald, lime, teal and warm wood colors. No Mario objects, no castle, no volcano goal, no logos. No large trees or structures in center. Maintain pixel detail of reference, no realism.
